# Ichno

**One-click technical drawings (PDF, SVG) from your 3D scene.** Free Blender add-on.

![Ichno](images/featured.png)

Orthographic and isometric views, dimensions and a full title block, exported as vector PDF and SVG,
ready to print or to open in Illustrator, Inkscape or a CAD tool.

## Install
- **Recommended:** Blender ‣ Edit ‣ Preferences ‣ Get Extensions, search for **Ichno**.
- Or download the zip from [Releases](../../releases) and use *Install from Disk*.

Requires Blender 4.3 or newer.

## Quick start
1. 3D Viewport ‣ Sidebar (N) ‣ **Ichno** tab.
2. Choose what to draw: visible objects, selection or one collection.
3. Click **Make Plan**. The PDF opens when it's done.

The drawing is computed by a second Blender in the background, from a copy of your file:
your scene is never modified, and you can cancel at any time.

## Features
- Front, side, top and isometric views; first- or third-angle projection
- Overall, dual and chain dimensions; coordinate ruler or architect grid
- A4 to A0, Letter, Tabloid, ANSI C/D; one sheet per collection, each with its own views
- Title block, revisions, custom fields, abbreviation legend, bill of materials
- Fast mode for drafts of heavy models
- English and French
